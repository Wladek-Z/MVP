Update move (i) samples configurations while conserving c because swapping the sign of 0 (He3) results in 0, and swapping the sign of +/- 1 results in -/+ 1, both of which are He4, hence never changing the fraction occupied by a species of molecule.

Update move (ii) also samples configurations while conserving c because swapping a pair of sites can only rearrange the molecules that already occupy the lattice. It is unable to create a new molecule of a certain species, or destroy one, without cancelling this action out at another site.

3. a. (normal fluid/mixed)

The dynamics appear random, with all spins uniformly distributed throughout the lattice.

3. b. (superfluid/mixed)

The system forms distinct patches of spin = +/- 1 (He4), with the He3 molecules uniformly distributed throughout. Furthermore, thermal fluctuations give rise to a uniform, but low density, distribution of unaligned spins.

3. c. (superfluid/demixed)

The system fully phase separates into 3 regions where the spins are aligned on average. The spin 0 sites form a layer that separates the spin 1 region from the spin -1 region.
